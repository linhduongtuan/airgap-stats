To assess the association between admission CRP concentration and 30-day mortality, we fit a crude logistic regression followed by an adjusted model including age, sex, bmi, treatment, sbp, diabetes, hypertension, smoking, egfr, hba1c, and complication. In the crude analysis, CRP was not significantly associated with mortality, with an odds ratio of 1.01 (95% CI: 0.97 to 1.05, p = 0.703). After adjustment for potential confounders, the odds ratio remained close to unity (OR 1.00, 95% CI: 0.96 to 1.05, p = 0.915). These results suggest that admission CRP concentration was not materially associated with 30-day mortality in this sample, both before and after adjusting for selected risk factors. Because this analysis is observational, the findings should be interpreted as adjusted associations rather than causal effects.
